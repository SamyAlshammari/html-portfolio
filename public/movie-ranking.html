My Best Movie Ranking

Down Below my best 3 movies of all time

1. The Shawshank Redemption

The Shawshank Redemption is a 1994 American drama film written and directed by Frank Darabont, based on the 1982 Stephen King novella Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption. It stars Tim Robbins and Morgan Freeman as two imprisoned men who bond over a number of years, finding solace and eventual redemption through acts of common decency.

2. The Godfather

The Godfather is a 1972 American crime film directed by Francis Ford Coppola, based on the 1969 novel of the same name by Mario Puzo. It stars Marlon Brando and Al Pacino as the heads of a fictional organized crime family.

3. The Dark Knight

The Dark Knight is a 2008 American superhero film directed by Christopher Nolan, based on the DC Comics character Batman. It stars Christian Bale as Bruce Wayne/Batman, Heath Ledger as the Joker, and Aaron Eckhart as Harvey Dent.
